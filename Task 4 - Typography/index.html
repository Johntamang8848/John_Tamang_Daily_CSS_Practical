Task 4 · Typography

Small choices make text easier to read

A short note on type, rhythm, and comfortable reading on screen.

Prepared by John Tamang

Good typography gives each part of a page a clear role. A strong heading helps readers find the subject, while a calm body font keeps longer passages comfortable.

Line length, spacing, and weight work together. When text has room to breathe, people can scan the page and return to details without losing their place.

“Typography is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form.”

A few useful habits

  • Choose a readable typeface for paragraphs.
  • Use weight and size to show hierarchy.
  • Keep line spacing generous enough for the text.
